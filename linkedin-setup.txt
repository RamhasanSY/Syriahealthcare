SYRIA HEALTHCARE - LINKEDIN PAGE SETUP

1. Create the Page while signed in to your own LinkedIn account:
   https://www.linkedin.com/company/setup/new/
   Choose Company. Enter the real organization type, size and other required details.
   Name: Syria Healthcare
   Website: https://syriahealthcare.com/
   Suggested public address: syriahealthcare (subject to availability)
   Tagline: Healthcare news and NGO opportunities in Syria.

About text:
Syria Healthcare is an independent information platform connecting people with
healthcare news and NGO opportunities in Syria. We collect original headlines
and current vacancies from publishers and employers, with links to the source.
Our website is available in English and Arabic. We do not represent the listed
employers; applications are handled by each employer.

First post draft (not published):
Welcome to Syria Healthcare.
Explore healthcare news and current healthcare and NGO opportunities in Syria,
with original sources, closing dates and employer application links.
Visit https://syriahealthcare.com/
#Syria #Healthcare #NGOJobs

2. Connect publishing:
   In Zapier, add a LinkedIn connection and complete LinkedIn's sign-in and
   authorization screen as an administrator of the Page.
   Trigger: RSS by Zapier - New Item in Feed
   Feed: https://syriahealthcare.com/data/news.xml
   Action: LinkedIn - Create Company Update
   Select the Syria Healthcare Page.
   Post text: feed Title, then Description (publisher attribution).
   Article URL: feed Link.
   Check a sample before enabling. Start with future new items, not the archive.
   The feed refreshes with the website's twice-daily collection. Zapier's polling
   and any account limits determine how soon a new item is posted.
   This is not connected yet; the account owner must authorize it in LinkedIn.
   Do not send passwords, browser cookies or access tokens in chat.

3. Keep your voice:
   Add a personal introduction to selected stories, or manually schedule Page
   posts inside LinkedIn. Automated posts should preserve publisher attribution.

4. Send the actual Page URL back so it can be linked from the website.
   Creating or connecting a Page does not enable importing LinkedIn jobs.
