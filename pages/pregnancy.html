PREGNANCY PLANNING

A healthy pregnancy, step by step.

General guidance based on the World Health Organization’s antenatal care recommendations. It does not replace advice from your doctor or midwife.

  1. 1

    Before pregnancy

    Have a check-up with a health worker. Start folic acid (400 µg a day), ideally before you conceive, and review your medicines and vaccinations.

  2. 2

    First trimester · weeks 1–12

    Book your first antenatal visit by week 12. Expect blood tests, a blood pressure check and confirmation of your due date. Keep taking folic acid.

  3. 3

    Second trimester · weeks 13–27

    Visits around weeks 20 and 26. WHO recommends one ultrasound scan before week 24. Ask about iron supplements and a healthy diet.

  4. 4

    Third trimester · weeks 28–40

    Visits around weeks 30, 34, 36, 38 and 40. Choose where you will give birth, plan transport and prepare your bag.

  5. 5

    After birth

    Postnatal checks for mother and baby within 24 hours, then on day 3, between days 7 and 14, and at 6 weeks.

Get care immediately if you have:

  • Vaginal bleeding
  • Convulsions or fits
  • Severe headache with blurred vision
  • Fever, or being too weak to get out of bed
  • Severe abdominal pain
  • Fast or difficult breathing
  • Baby moving less than usual
