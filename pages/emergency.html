EMERGENCY NUMBERS

Emergency numbers in Syria.

On a phone, tap a number to call it. If you cannot get through, go straight to the nearest hospital.

  • 🚑

    Ambulance

    Call the ambulance: 110

    Medical emergencies. The Ministry of Health’s single ambulance number for every governorate.

  • 🚓

    Police

    Call the police: 112

    Crime and threats to your safety. If someone is hurt, also call the ambulance.

  • 🚒

    Fire

    Call the fire service: 113

    Fires and smoke.

Emergency services can be limited in some areas. Keep the number of your nearest hospital on your phone.
